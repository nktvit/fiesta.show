Plays the average of left and right in both ears. Useful with one earbud or for hearing loss in one ear.

For headphones: lets a little of each channel reach the other ear, softened and slightly delayed, like speakers do.

Scales the difference between left and right. 0 is mono, 1 leaves the mix alone, 2 is extra wide.
