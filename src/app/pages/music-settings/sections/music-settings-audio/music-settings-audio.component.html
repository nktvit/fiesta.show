How the equalizer works
  • Graphic is the simple one: one slider per frequency, plus presets you can save.
  • Parametric gives each band its own frequency, gain and Q. Low and high shelves boost or cut everything below or above a frequency.
  • Mid/Side bands act only on what is the same in both channels (mid, usually vocals and bass) or only on what differs (side, the width).
  • AutoEQ compares a measurement of your headphones with a target curve and places bands that cancel the difference.
  • Lower the preamp when you boost, otherwise loud passages can clip. AutoEQ sets it for you.
